Powerful Features

Everything you need to promote and fund your open source project

🤝

Sponsor Outreach

Discover potential sponsors on GitHub, craft personalized messages, and reach out via email or forums with rate limiting and cooldowns.

📝

Content Syndication

Write once, publish everywhere. Syndicate articles to DEV, Hashnode, Medium, Ghost, Bluesky, and more with automated publishing.

🛡️

Ethical & Compliant

Built-in quality gates, disclosure requirements, rate limits, and anti-spam measures to maintain community trust.

⚡

Dependency-Light

Only 2 runtime dependencies (PyYAML, requests). Runs anywhere with Python 3.10+. Optional local web UI with zero extra deps.

🔒

Secure by Design

Secrets from environment variables only. Atomic writes, crash-safe persistence, and secure local web UI with 2FA support.

🎯

Campaign Management

Manage multiple projects with isolated profiles. Track outreach status, publication history, and analytics in one place.

How It Works

Two powerful pipelines working together

Outreach Pipeline

  1. Discover - Find potential sponsors on GitHub/GitLab by topics
  2. Track - Manage prospects with status and cooldowns
  3. Generate - Craft personalized messages with AI or templates
  4. Send - Reach out via email or forums at safe rates

Syndication Pipeline

  1. Draft - Write articles with quality gates and disclosure
  2. Approve - Human review before publishing
  3. Publish - Auto-post to APIs or get paste-ready files
  4. Track - Monitor live URLs across all platforms
